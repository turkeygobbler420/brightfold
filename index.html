Process

Scoring

Where scores fall on the curve

IQ scales are normed so the population mean is 100 and one standard deviation is 15 points. About two thirds of people land between 85 and 115.

  • 130 +Very high≈ 2.3%
  • 115 – 129Above average≈ 13.6%
  • 85 – 114Average≈ 68.2%
  • 70 – 84Below average≈ 13.6%
  • under 70Low≈ 2.3%

Brightfold’s number is a practice estimate mapped onto this scale. It has not been normed on a sample population and is not a clinical assessment.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Is this a real IQ test?

It uses the same item types as recognised tests, but a proper IQ score needs a test normed on thousands of people and given under controlled conditions. Treat your result as a practice estimate.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no sign-up, no payment and no certificate upsell. Your answers stay in your browser tab and are not sent anywhere.

Why six domains?

Modern batteries such as the WAIS report separate indexes for verbal comprehension, fluid reasoning, visual-spatial ability, working memory and processing speed. Brightfold adds number series as a quantitative strand.

Can I go back and change answers?

Yes, for every item except the memory and speed tasks, which you only see once. Those depend on a single exposure to be meaningful.

Can I use a calculator or paper?

Paper is fine. A calculator gives you an advantage on the series items, so your score will read higher than it should.

What happens if time runs out?

The test ends and unanswered items count as wrong. You still get the full breakdown and every explanation.

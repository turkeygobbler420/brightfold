FAQ

Answers to common questions

What is included in the membership?
A personal IQ report with a certificate, every assessment, brain games, short courses and progress tracking. Everything syncs between devices when you sign in.
How do I end my membership?
Open the cancellation page (footer, under Support), enter your email, type the six-digit code and confirm. You keep access until the period you paid for runs out.
Who can see my results?
Only you, unless you decide to share them. Rankings are anonymous, and you can erase your data whenever you like.
What counts as a strong score?
100 is the average and most people land between 85 and 115. A score of 130 or more puts you in roughly the top 2%.
Can my score go up?
It can, through schooling, regular mental exercise, enough sleep and good health. Practice also builds memory, focus and pattern spotting.
Where do I find my results afterwards?
In your account, under Report and Certificate. Once unlocked, you can save the full report as a PDF.
Does IQ shift as people get older?
It tends to stay fairly steady but can drift. Thinking speed may slow with age, while vocabulary and knowledge often keep growing.
Is intelligence passed down from parents?
Genes contribute, but they do not decide everything. Schooling, health, surroundings and life experience shape thinking too.

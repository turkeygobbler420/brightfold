How it works

    Answer twenty questions

Questions people ask first

Is this a real IQ test?
It uses the same item types as recognised tests, but a proper IQ score needs a test normed on thousands of people and given under controlled conditions. Treat your result as a practice estimate.
Does it cost anything?
No. There is no sign-up, no payment and no paywall on your result or your certificate. Your answers stay in your browser tab and are not sent anywhere.
How long does it take?
Sixteen minutes at most, for 20 questions. The clock pauses on the checkpoint screens, so reading them never costs you time.
Why six domains?
Modern batteries such as the WAIS report separate indexes for verbal comprehension, fluid reasoning, visual-spatial ability, working memory and processing speed. Brightfold adds number series as a quantitative strand.
How is the score calculated?
Your share of correct answers is mapped onto a mean-100, standard-deviation-15 scale. Because the items have not been normed on a sample population, the number is a rough guide, not a clinical result.
Can I go back and change answers?
Yes, for every question except the memory and speed tasks, which you only see once. Those depend on a single exposure to be meaningful.
Can I use a calculator or paper?
Paper is fine. A calculator gives you an advantage on the series questions, so your score will read higher than it should.
What happens if time runs out?
The test ends and unanswered questions count as wrong. You still get the full breakdown and every explanation.
What does the certificate say?
It shows your name, estimated score, date and the practice-scale disclaimer. It is a keepsake for finishing the test, not an official credential.
Is my data stored?
No. Everything runs in your browser. Close the tab and your answers are gone, so save your certificate before you leave.

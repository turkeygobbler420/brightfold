Questions, answered.

What does my subscription include?

Are my results private?
Yes. Your results are visible only to you unless you choose to share them. Rankings are anonymous, and you can delete your account and personal data at any time.
What is a good IQ score?
The average IQ score is 100, and most people score between 85 and 115. A score above 100 is above average, while 130 or higher is within approximately the top 2%.
Can you increase your IQ score?
Your score can improve with education, regular mental practice, quality sleep and good health. Training can also strengthen skills such as memory, focus and pattern recognition.
Where do I find my results?
Your IQ score and certificate are available in your profile. Once unlocked, you can download your report as a PDF.
Does IQ change with age?
IQ is generally stable, but it can change over time. Processing speed may decline with age, while vocabulary and accumulated knowledge can continue to grow.
Do you inherit your IQ from your parents?
Genetics play a role, but IQ is not determined by genes alone. Education, health, environment and life experiences also influence cognitive development.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your email address, your quiz answers and resulting score, and basic technical data (browser, device, approximate region) needed to run the service. Payments are handled by our third-party billing provider; we do not see or store your card number.

How we use it

To calculate and show your IQ score and report, to run your account and subscription, to send service emails you can opt out of, and to measure and improve the site.

Sharing

We share data only with providers that help us operate the service (hosting, authentication, billing, analytics and advertising measurement) and when the law requires it. We do not sell your quiz answers.

Your choices

You can request access to or deletion of your data, and unsubscribe from emails at any time. Contact us using the details on the Contact page.

Retention

We keep account data while your account is active and for the period required for billing and legal records, then delete or anonymise it.
